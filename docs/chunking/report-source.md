# Chunking strategy research source ledger

Canonical research notes for `chunking_strategy.md`. Research cutoff: 2026-08-23.

| Claim family | Source | Evidence and applicability | Confidence / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieval granularity is task- and budget-dependent | [Dense X Retrieval: What Retrieval Granularity Should We Use?](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.845/) — Chen et al., EMNLP 2024 | On five Wikipedia open-QA datasets, self-contained propositions outperformed passages/sentences most clearly at 100–200 retrieved-word budgets; effects converge with more budget. | High for its evaluated setting; do not generalize to all corpus types. Proposition extraction is expensive and imperfect. |
| Fine chunks need wider context | [Late Chunking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04701) — Günther et al., 2024, revised 2025 | Pooling long-context token embeddings over chunk spans improved mean nDCG@10 by 1.5–1.9 points across its three models/four BEIR tasks; its overlap ablation found no clear advantage. | Moderate: author preprint and long-context embedding requirement. |
| Hierarchy can solve precision/completeness tension | [HiChunk](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1372/) — Lu et al., ACL 2026 | Builds multi-level boundaries and retrieves fine chunks, then conditionally merges to parents. On its evidence-dense HiCBench T1, authors report Qwen3-32B evidence recall 74.06 to 81.03 and Fact-Cov 63.20 to 68.12 for fixed 200-token vs hierarchical-200 + Auto-Merge. Sparse-evidence benchmarks show smaller differences. | High that the evaluation issue and pattern merit testing; moderate that a fine-tuned LLM hierarchy is justified for QMD. |
| Query-dependent granularity can help | [Mix-of-Granularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00456) — Zhong et al., COLING 2025 | A router selects among pre-indexed granularities, evaluated on medical QA. | Moderate. Multiple indexes and router latency/incorrect routing increase complexity. |
| Query-adaptive windows are promising | [QASC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22834) — Rastogi, 2026 | Seed sentences by query similarity, expand local windows, merge/score query-specific chunks. | Low-to-moderate: single-author preprint, custom small corpus, and a query-count inconsistency (abstract says 200, body/results say 40). Treat as a hypothesis. |
| Broad questions benefit from abstraction levels | [RAPTOR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.18059) — Sarthi et al., ICLR 2024 | Recursively clusters/summarizes to enable document-scale retrieval. | Moderate. Summary nodes are lossy; preserve raw-leaf provenance and never use them as final evidence. |
| Contextual metadata can be a practical alternative | [Contextual Retrieval](https://www.anthropic.com/news/contextual-retrieval) — Anthropic, 2024 | Adds a short document-specific context to each chunk for dense and BM25 indexing. Anthropic reports lower top-20 failure rates with contextual embeddings/BM25/reranking. | Low-to-moderate: vendor benchmark; validate cost and quality locally. |
| Chunking needs multi-axis evaluation | [RAGBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11005) — Friel et al., 2024/2025 | TRACe distinguishes context relevance, utilization, grounded adherence, and completeness. | High for evaluation design, not a proof of any splitter’s performance. |
| Practical statistical semantic segmentation | [Semantic Chunkers](https://github.com/aurelio-labs/semantic-chunkers) — Aurelio AI, accessed 2026-08-23 | Its `StatisticalChunker` compares each segment embedding with a local-window mean and searches a similarity threshold that targets a bounded median chunk size. | Implementation source, not comparative research evidence. MIT-licensed; adapt the algorithm with attribution and validate it on QMD corpora. |

Additional related work: Nguyen et al.’s [Enhancing Retrieval Augmented Generation with Hierarchical Text Segmentation Chunking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09935) (2025) reports modest gains from segment+cluster representations on long-document QA. The related Springer chapter [with the same topic](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-96-4288-5_17) was located, but its full text was not reliably accessible; no performance claim from it is used.

## Reconciliation

The papers disagree in absolute wins because their corpora, retrievers, prompt budgets, and evidence density differ. That is not a contradiction: it establishes that chunking is a corpus-and-query-distribution decision. The least speculative design shared by the evidence is to preserve source structure, retrieve small self-contained leaves, and recover sufficient local/parent context only after retrieval. Test advanced semantic, hierarchical, and query-adaptive variants against that baseline at equal final-context and latency budgets.

## Repository-specific corpus note: agent journals

For daily agent-memory Markdown, the file/date is a provenance boundary rather than a semantic one. The implementation strategy should parse session, timestamp, speaker, typed-fact, and event boundaries before generic Markdown/token chunking; index atomic events with their local metadata; and expand only within the same event/session when evidence requires it. This is an application of the source-backed small-unit-plus-parent pattern, tailored to the corpus rather than a separate research claim.
